About

I study buildings and build brands.

I'm an architecture student at the University of Miami who fell straight into the trap of Miami entrepreneurship. Studio taught me that everything has a reason: every surface answers to a structure, and every detail argues for the whole. Reaching an audience works the same way. The post, the caption, the campaign are all surfaces, and they have to answer to something underneath — a strategy, a point of view, a reason to care. Get the structure right and the details start arguing for you. The studio work is where that thinking was trained.

It started with a photography dump and an account called pizza.man.photo. I've since moved to a more professional handle, but that foundation is what led everywhere else: brand voice, launch content and organic growth at UTern; the full brand system for APEX STUDENT, from the website and the blog to the mascot and the UI; and Stamped, a travel concept about treating content like a luxury product. Along the way I scaled a friend's Instagram account past 30,000 followers, and made "Bouquet for the Rain," which won the 2026 Michele Edelson Photography Award at the University of Miami's Annual Juried Student Art Exhibition.

The part I didn't expect is the part I'm proudest of. At UTern the voice kept needing surfaces that didn't exist — there was no blog, no newsletter, and the analytics couldn't tell us whether a student had actually signed up. Rather than file tickets and wait, I learned the codebase. Twelve pull requests later, those things exist: a blog with an editor, a newsletter with real double opt-in running on Resend, and measurement that finally reports the truth. It's all on GitHub, and it's the reason I now describe myself as someone who markets a product and then ships it.

Studio sharpens how I think; startups sharpen how fast I move. One teaches you to draw the whole system before the first line, the other to ship before the moment passes. I'm looking for the next real thing to build — early-stage teams who need someone to own brand and content end to end and who won't be stopped by the engineering queue.
